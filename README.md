## 01. Brand Identity & Creative Concept

**AARNA** is an independent contemporary Indian luxury fashion atelier. The house unites classical Indian unstitched drape geometry (Varanasi Kadwa silks, Chanderi weaves, and Jaipur marodi work) with modern architectural tailoring and restraint.

### Visual Palette
* **Warm Ivory** (`#F4F0E8`) — Soft architectural backdrop.
* **Deep Forest** (`#193C32`) — Primary brand grounding tone.
* **Muted Olive** (`#8C9278`) — Subtle botanical accents.
* **Soft Sandstone** (`#C7B8A2`) — Sedimentary plinths and gallery flooring.
* **Espresso** (`#302720`) — Deep typographic contrast.

## 03. Independent 3D Spatial Capabilities

* **Parametric Garment Geometries**: Original mathematical formulations generating contemporary Indian silhouettes:
  * *Kashi Pre-Pleated Saree* — Pre-sculpted architectural pleats with a continuous cascading 3D pallu.
  * *Chandrika Flared Lehenga* — 24-kali radial skirt flare with undulating harmonic waves.
  * *Meera Fluted Anarkali* — High-waist fluted bell gown with knife-pleated flare.
  * *Surya Tailored Co-ord* — Structured matka silk bandhgala blazer over fluid drape trousers.
  * *Pallavi Cowl Gown* — Asymmetrical one-shoulder bias-cut silk crepe drape.
  * *Tara Angrakha Tunic* — Crossover overlapping front panels with side ties and brass tassels.
* **Textile & Material Shader Work**:
  * Pure Mulberry Silk (`MeshPhysicalMaterial` with anisotropic sheen and micro-slubs).
  * Gossamer Handloom Chanderi (semi-sheer with micro-glint metallic highlights).
  * Coarse Handspun Matka Raw Silk (coarse organic slub relief).
  * Heavy Satin Silk Crepe (liquid bias-cut draping).
  * Aged Hand-beaten Brass Hardware (`#C5A059`).
* **Interactive Inspection**: Selecting any garment triggers a smooth camera focus transition, allowing 360° mouse and touch orbit rotation, colorway switching, bespoke sizing, and direct addition to the persistent Atelier Bag.
